## 3. Design Direction

### Mood
- **Optimistic futurism**
- Sophisticated, not childish: **no bubbly Y2K overload**
- Optional **"aero mode"** toggle (?)

### Look & Feel
- Glassmorphism
- Soft gradients
- Geometric fonts, for contrast with the soft visuals
- Pixels
- Line borders that **resize depending on the content being shown**
- Flickering content
- Holographic card effects on hover

### Colour
- White, black and blue, with the ability to **switch between them**
